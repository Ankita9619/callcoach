# Title: Internet outage – dismissive handling
# Agent: Mark
Agent: Yeah, support.
Customer: Hi, my internet has been down since yesterday evening and I work from home. This is really unacceptable.
Agent: Did you restart the router?
Customer: Yes, three times. The light is red.
Agent: Well, you should have checked the outage page before calling. There's work in your area.
Customer: I did check it, it says there's no outage. This is ridiculous.
Agent: Look, calm down. There's nothing I can do if the system says there's no outage.
Customer: Can you at least send a technician?
Agent: You'd have to call back later, the technician team is busy.
Customer: This is a complete waste of time. I want to make a complaint.
Agent: Okay. Bye.
